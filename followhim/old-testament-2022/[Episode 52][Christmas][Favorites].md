John Bytheway: 01:08 And it’s an important part and I love it and I watch the Christmas shows like everyone else. Hank Smith: 01:28 So level one.

John Bytheway: 01:29 Yeah, level one is Santa Claus.

Hank Smith: 01:31 Level two.

John Bytheway: 01:32 Is level two is Silent Night level.

Hank Smith: 01:33 Silent Night. I’ve heard someone say before, John, approaching the Lord of the universe can be intimidating for some people. And we’re not here talking about it unless there was the level three of Christmas. And that’s the Lord level, he called it. We might even call it Easter. The resurrected Christ who performed his work, suffered for our sins, our problems, our infirmities, everything because of him we’ll all live again. And we wouldn’t have level one or two if it weren’t for that level three.

02:47 It’s kind of fun. Hank Smith: 03:09 Right. I like that level three, John, because this is what makes Christ different than anybody else. A lot of people have been born but nobody’s been resurrected.
