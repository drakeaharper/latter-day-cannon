01:01                   I remember watching Andrew so determined, he was three, to climb to the top of this jungle gym. He was struggling and he’d climb and then he’d come down. Here I am watching the whole thing. And then he’d try again. I kept thinking, should I run over there and help him? The smarter part of me, maybe some inspiration was no. Let him do it. Let him struggle. Let him try. Just think of this verse of scripture, right? Let’s see what they will do maybe when life is hard. I kept wanting to help and then staying back and it took him a long time, but he finally got to the top. Even a three-year-old has a look of satisfaction and of joy. A lot of times, you know, we hear questions, “Well, why didn’t the Lord just put us where we’re gonna end up anyway?” Well, there’s a lot of growth and a lot of struggle and a lot of triumph even that comes with seeing what we will do.

01:58                   And the whole verse will prove them to see if they will do that if, means, yeah, there’s agency. We get to choose what we will do. Sometimes the Lord lets us struggle and wrestle to see what we will do. And sometimes we have those moments of, “Oh, I did this with the Lord’s help. Not one of the trials and tribulations we face is beyond our limits because we have access to help from the Lord.” So there it is. We’ll prove them now herewith to see what they will do.
